# AIscanAuto

AIscanAuto is a free AI-powered OBD2 car diagnostic application for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## What it does

- Reads OBD2 fault codes (DTCs) from any ELM327 adapter
- Provides AI-generated explanations and repair guidance for diagnostic trouble codes
- Monitors live engine data (PIDs) in real time
- Supports 99% of cars manufactured from 1996 onwards

## Key features

- AI diagnostics: natural-language explanations of complex fault codes
- Live data streaming: real-time sensor readings and PID monitoring
- Multi-platform: desktop apps for Windows, macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon), and Linux
- ELM327 compatibility: works with Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and USB OBD2 adapters
- Free tier available with paid plans for advanced features

## Target audience

- Car owners performing DIY diagnostics
- Auto repair shops and mechanics
- Automotive enthusiasts and tuners

## Languages supported

English, German, Russian, Polish, Spanish, Italian
